Acadia University to Host the 2027 Atlantic AI Summit

Acadia University is proud to announce that it has been selected as the host institution for the 2027 Atlantic AI Summit. Through the Acadia Institute for Data Analytics (AIDA) we look forward to welcoming researchers, students, industry leaders, entrepreneurs, government partners, and the broader AI community from across Atlantic Canada and beyond for three days of collaboration, innovation, and connection. We are excited to showcase Acadia’s growing leadership in applied artificial intelligence while providing a welcoming environment for conversations and partnerships that will help shape the future of AI in our region.

Each year, AI Atlantic brings together academics, industry leaders, and government partners from across the region for a multi-day summit celebrating AI research, collaboration, and innovation in Atlantic Canada.

Further details on the 2027 Summit, including program information, and registration, will be available soon at this address.

June 9-11, 2027 Wolfville, Nova Scotia
